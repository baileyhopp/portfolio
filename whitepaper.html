The Hidden Cost of Disconnected Data

Sample executive summary and outline · Fictional research · Placeholder content
This is a format sample. Any statistics are illustrative only. Do not publish invented figures; swap in sourced data.

Executive summary

Mid-market companies run on dozens of tools, and most of those tools hold a different version of the truth. Teams compensate with spreadsheets, manual exports and status meetings. The cost rarely appears on a budget line, so it rarely gets fixed.

This paper makes three arguments. First, disconnected data creates recurring labor that scales with headcount. Second, it slows decisions because leaders cannot trust the first number they see. Third, the fix does not require a platform overhaul: a small number of well-chosen integrations removes most of the pain.

Key takeaways

  • Manual data handling is a hidden operating cost that grows as the company grows.
  • Conflicting numbers erode trust in reporting and delay decisions.
  • Start with the three reports leaders use most, then work backward to the systems behind them.

Sample excerpt: section 4 opening

You do not need to connect everything. Begin with the reports your leadership team opens every week. Trace each number back to its source, note every manual step in between, and rank those steps by hours consumed. The top three are your starting point. Fixing them usually delivers most of the benefit and builds the case for what comes next.
