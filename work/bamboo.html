I started scaling the Efficient Growth community in October 2022. By January 2023, my team and I had grown it from 428 to over 1,700 members, more than tripling it in one quarter.

What I did

  • Launched community building for Efficient Growth, a private, invite-only Slack community for marketers.
  • Organized in-person and virtual networking events, from happy hours to co-marketing events with partners, plus monthly Community Chat webinars featuring community members.
  • Launched the weekly Efficient Growth Digest newsletter, highlighting industry news and the best community exchanges of the week.
  • Supported the paid media team and lead gen efforts: reviewed new member requests, wrote ad copy, and reported weekly on growth, engagement, and newsletter sign-ups.
  • Used social listening to surface community insights for product and sales teams.
  • Owned Bamboo's social profiles on LinkedIn and Twitter, and the company website, including blog posts and case studies.
  • Led partnership efforts with companies including Rockerbox, Branch, TikTok, Meta, Google, and Klaviyo, including guest blogs, webinars, and co-marketing.
