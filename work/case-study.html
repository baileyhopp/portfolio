The challenge

Northbridge Freight is a regional logistics company with six depots. Every Monday, analysts pulled data from four systems into spreadsheets, reconciled mismatches by hand and emailed a summary to depot managers. The process took most of a day, and errors regularly reached leadership.

"By the time we saw the numbers, the week was already half over."Operations Manager, Northbridge Freight (placeholder quote)

The solution

The team replaced the manual reporting routine with an automated pipeline. Data from each system is pulled on a schedule, mismatches are flagged for review instead of silently corrected, and the finished report is delivered to managers before they start work on Monday.

  • Week one: mapped the existing process and picked the highest-effort report to automate first.
  • Week two: launched the first workflow and ran it in parallel with the manual process to build trust.
  • Weeks three to twelve: rolled the approach out to eleven more teams.
